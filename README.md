# Hi, I'm Fabrizio Palladino

I'm a front-end developer and UX engineer based in Brussels. I work on design systems, accessible interfaces, and the connection between design and development.

I enjoy understanding how people work, designing systems that make their work easier, and staying close to building the solution.

## Front-end and design systems

My experience includes introducing a component-based approach to website design and development, writing guidelines adopted by designers, and helping editors build pages using reusable components.

The work I care about connects:

- **Design and development:** shared component models and clearer collaboration.
- **Accessibility and usability:** semantic HTML, CSS, and interfaces people can use.
- **Front-end architecture:** reusable components and maintainable JavaScript and TypeScript.

## What I'm building and exploring

I'm building a personal job-search tool with TypeScript and Node.js. It stores job postings, assesses them against a local profile, and helps draft applications grounded in my experience. It gives me a practical setting to explore AI workflows, evidence, and human review.

I'm also learning about AI agents and how they can help with everyday work. I'm interested in where they are useful, how to check their output, and which decisions should stay with the person using them.
